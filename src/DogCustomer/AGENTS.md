- This project adds one dog customer to Probably Stolen using its native mod loader. Preserve all original customers, save data, and game files.
- Treat the locally installed game's assemblies as version-specific evidence; do not distribute or replace them.
- The character's spoken lines consist exclusively of the character 旺 and punctuation. Visits are additive, probabilistic, and at most once per opening; starting buying budget is 100–300 inclusive.
